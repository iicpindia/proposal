Student Data & Consent

Interview Champ runs entirely in the student's browser — their answers and results never leave their device. Student data for the Fast Forward to HR module is processed only on the institute's written authorisation. The institute remains the data owner and confirms it has obtained the required student consents; IICP acts solely as a data processor, using the information only for this HR outreach module and only for the duration of the engagement.
